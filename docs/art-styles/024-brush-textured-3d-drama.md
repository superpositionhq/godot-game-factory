# Brush-textured 3D drama

Reference works: Arcane.

## Visual definition

- **Shape language and proportions:** Realistic anatomy with slightly sharpened planes on faces and props. Architecture is dense and ornate with art-deco and industrial detail.
- **Line and outline:** Occasional dark painted accents at creases and under forms; no continuous outline.
- **Shading model:** Light is broken into four to six painted tone planes with visible stroke edges. Highlights are drawn as distinct brush marks.
- **Palette:** `#1f2a44` `#c2553f` `#e3a857` `#3e8e8c` `#7a3e65` `#d9cdb8` `#12141c`. Dark teal-blue shadow against warm orange and gold light; magenta and green accents.
- **Lighting:** Dramatic, low-key: strong warm practicals and cool fill, hard rim lights.
- **Texture and surface:** Every surface looks painted: directional brush strokes follow the form, with scumbled edges and painted speculars.
- **Atmosphere and post-processing:** Painted-looking smoke and glow drawn as 2D shapes; light bloom; subtle film grain.
- **Camera and framing:** Cinematic, shallow depth, 50 to 85 mm equivalent for characters, wide for cities.
- **UI and typography:** Art-deco frames with thin gold lines and angular corner ornaments, and painted icons with visible strokes. Titles use a tall condensed deco sans; damage numbers are rough hand-painted numerals. UI colours are gold `#e3a857` and cream `#d9cdb8` on near-black `#12141c`, with magenta or teal accents.
- **VFX and motion:** Smoke, sparks and explosions are hand-painted 2D shapes animated on twos over full-rate 3D motion; hits flash with magenta or teal painted bursts. Character motion is weighty and realistic, with sharp accents on impacts.

## Fits

- **Genres:** `action-adventure`, `third-person-shooter`, `fighting`, `stealth`, `detective-mystery`, `rpg`
- **Moods:** dramatic, gritty, tense
- **Settings:** steampunk undercity, art-deco upper city, industrial slums, back-alley laboratories

## Godot recipe

### Assets

Realistic-anatomy characters and dense, ornate architecture with art-deco and industrial detail. Keep geometry detailed, but repaint every texture: in a generator script (`Image`) run a directional smear along the UV-space tangent flow, then stamp brush strokes that follow the surface direction, with scumbled edges. Keep normal maps but replace their fine detail with a stroke-relief normal generated from the stroke texture. Generate a brush-stroke tile and painted highlight-mark textures in the same script. 2048 px for hero assets.

### Materials

Write `style/brush_drama.gdshader`. Its `light()` takes `dot(NORMAL, LIGHT) * ATTENUATION` for each light and breaks it into four to six tone planes: quantise the value after offsetting it with the stroke texture sampled along the UVs (passed from `fragment()`), so each plane boundary is a visible stroke edge. Colour the planes from a ramp texture that runs dark teal-blue (`#1f2a44`) to warm orange and gold, written to `DIFFUSE_LIGHT`. Specular is drawn as distinct marks: threshold the highlight and multiply by a stroke mask so it appears as separate dabs in `SPECULAR_LIGHT`. Add a hard rim term (a stepped `1.0 - dot(NORMAL, VIEW)`). No continuous outline; dark accents at creases come from the occlusion map multiplied into `ALBEDO`.

### Lighting and atmosphere

Low-key and dramatic: warm practical `OmniLight3D` and `SpotLight3D` lights, a cool dim fill, hard rim lights behind characters. Forward+ handles many lights; shadows on the main practicals. Bake a `LightmapGI` for a cool base. Dark teal `Environment` fog at low density. SSAO on, medium. Reflection probes at reduced intensity. On the Mobile renderer: keep each mesh within 8 omni and 8 spot lights, and replace SSAO with the baked lightmap's occlusion plus the occlusion map already in the albedo.

### Post-processing

A Kuwahara filter with a 5 to 6 px radius, masked by depth so near characters keep detail: a full-screen `QuadMesh` in front of the camera whose spatial shader reads `hint_screen_texture` and `hint_depth_texture`. Then light glow, subtle film grain in a `ColorRect` shader on a `CanvasLayer`, far and near depth-of-field blur on `CameraAttributesPractical` for character shots, `ACES` tonemapping, and a colour-correction 3D LUT generated by script that splits toward teal shadows and orange highlights. TAA. On the Mobile renderer: SMAA instead of TAA.

### UI and effects

Art-deco frames with thin gold lines and angular corner ornaments (`StyleBoxFlat` borders plus corner textures); icons painted with visible strokes. Titles from a tall condensed deco face such as Poiret One or Limelight; damage numbers from a rough painted face such as Rock Salt. `#e3a857` and `#d9cdb8` on `#12141c`. Smoke, sparks and explosions are hand-painted flipbooks (`particles_anim_h_frames`, `particles_anim_v_frames`) stepped at 12 frames per second over full-rate 3D motion; hits flash a magenta or teal painted burst.

## Do / Do not

Do:

- Orient strokes along the form.
- Break tone boundaries along stroke edges.
- Paint highlights as discrete marks.
- Light with warm practicals against cool fill.

Do not:

- Do not blur strokes away into broad blocks; that reads as `021`.
- Do not light evenly with saturated zone colours; that reads as `023`.
- Do not cover the frame with thick swirling strokes and outlines; that reads as `076`.
- Do not add halftone dots; that reads as `009`.

## Nearest styles

- `021` Brush-block martial painterly: this style shows directional brush strokes and painted highlight marks on every surface; `021` smooths them into broad soft tone blocks with no small marks.
- `028` Expressionist oil isometric: strokes here follow the form in an ordered way under dramatic warm and cool light; `028` has thicker paint, short strokes running in many directions, unmixed colours and flat overcast light.
- `026` Oily concept-art realism: here light breaks into stepped planes with stroke edges and strong colour; `026` has smooth gradients and a greyer look.
