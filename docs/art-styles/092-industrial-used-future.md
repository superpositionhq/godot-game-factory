## Visual definition

- **Shape language and proportions:** Functional industrial spacecraft interiors: low corridors with ribbed walls, exposed pipes and conduits, bulkhead doors, grated floors, chunky analogue control panels with small CRT screens and rows of square buttons.
- **Line and outline:** No outline.
- **Shading model:** Physically based and smooth, low-key, with soft specular on worn painted metal.
- **Palette:** `#0f1412` `#2b332f` `#59645c` `#8a8f80` `#c9b458` `#a8402a` `#3f6f6a`. Dark grey-green and off-white painted metal, with amber warning lights, dull red stencils and teal monitor glow.
- **Lighting:** Dim, practical: strip lights behind grilles, flashing amber beacons, monitor glow, steam-diffused back light. Many dark corners.
- **Texture and surface:** Worn paint, scuffs on edges, stencilled labels and hazard stripes, padded wall sections, condensation, grime in recesses.
- **Atmosphere and post-processing:** Steam and haze, film grain, slight anamorphic flare, CRT scanlines on screens.
- **Camera and framing:** Low, wide lens in tight corridors; slow tracking.
- **UI and typography:** Chunky CRT-style readouts in teal or amber phosphor on black, set in bevelled metal bezels with hazard-stripe edges; icons are blocky pictograms like stencilled ship labels. Type is a squared low-resolution monospace face, with condensed stencil capitals for labels. Counters are segmented digits in amber `#c9b458`, with dull red `#a8402a` for alarms.
- **VFX and motion:** Steam bursts from pipes, sparks from damaged panels, flickering strip lights and rotating amber beacons; hits throw sparks and a brief flash into the haze. Motion is weighty and realistic, with heavy slow doors and grounded, deliberate character animation.

## Godot recipe

### Assets

Industrial spacecraft interiors: low ribbed corridors, exposed pipes and conduits, bulkhead doors, grated floors, chunky analogue control panels with small CRT screens and rows of square buttons. Sci-fi industrial packs fit well. Keep PBR maps and add wear in a generator script with `Image`: edge scuffs from a curvature mask, grime in recesses from the occlusion map, condensation patches in roughness. Add stencilled labels and hazard stripes as `Decal` nodes with textures generated by script. Recolour paint to dark grey-green and off-white.

### Materials

`StandardMaterial3D` with worn painted metal: metallic low under paint and high where scuffed (a metallic texture), roughness 0.5 to 0.7 for a soft specular. Padded sections use cloth-like high roughness. CRT screens use a written spatial shader, `crt_screen.gdshader`, with `render_mode unshaded`: teal or amber phosphor graphics with scanlines, a slight barrel curve on `UV`, flicker from `TIME`, and colour above 1 so it glows. Labels and stripes are `Decal` nodes. Beacons and strip lights use emission. No outline.

### Lighting and atmosphere

Dim and practical. Strip lights behind grilles (emission plus baked lights or low-energy `SpotLight3D` lights with projector textures), rotating amber beacons (a `SpotLight3D` rotated by script, shadows on), teal monitor glow from small `OmniLight3D` lights, and backlight through steam. Use Forward+. Bake `LightmapGI` at low energy so corners stay dark but not empty. A `ReflectionProbe` per corridor section. Depth fog in grey-green at low density, plus steam particles. SSAO on, strong. No directional light. On the Mobile renderer: there is no SSAO, so bake strong occlusion into the lightmap and occlusion maps, and keep at most eight omni and eight spot lights reaching any one mesh.

### Post-processing

`ACES` tonemapping, low exposure. Medium film grain and a 0.3 vignette from a `ColorRect` on a `CanvasLayer` with a `canvas_item` shader reading `hint_screen_texture`. Slight anamorphic flare: thin horizontal additive streak quads, billboarded, on beacons and screens at low intensity. Mild glow on beacons and screens. Colour stays muted: adjustment saturation 0.9. TAA. On the Mobile renderer: TAA is missing, so use MSAA 4x.

### UI and effects

Chunky CRT-style readouts in teal or amber on black inside bevelled metal bezels (`StyleBoxTexture` 9-slice) with hazard-stripe edges; the same scanline treatment as world screens, as a `canvas_item` shader on the readout. Font imported from a squared low-resolution monospace such as VT323 or Share Tech Mono, with condensed stencil capitals such as Saira Stencil One for labels; segmented digits in `#c9b458`, `#a8402a` for alarms. Icons are blocky pictograms. Effects: steam bursts from pipes (`GPUParticles3D` with a shaded material), spark showers from panels, strip-light flicker by animating emission energy and `light_energy` together, hits that throw sparks and a brief light flash into the haze. Doors are slow and heavy.

## Do / Do not

Do:

- Fill walls with ribs, pipes and analogue panels.
- Light with dim practical strips and amber beacons.
- Add stencil labels and hazard stripes.
- Add steam and haze.

Do not:

- Do not use clean bright white panels; that reads as `091`.
- Do not use bone-like organic ribbing in a single grey; that reads as `094`.
- Do not use domestic decay with a flashlight; that reads as `029`.
- Do not add neon signs and rain; that reads as `089`.

## Nearest styles

- `029` Survival-horror dark photoreal: everything here is manufactured (ribbed metal walls, control panels, stencil labels) and dimly lit by strips and beacons; `029` is darker still, lit mainly by a flashlight, and shows domestic rot, mould and peeling surfaces.
- `094` Biomechanical monochrome: this style is painted metal with flat labelled panels and amber, red and teal lights; `094` has no colour at all, and every surface is grown into bone-grey ribs and tubes.
- `089` Rain-soaked tech-noir: colour here is muted grey-green with only small warning lights; `089` is a wet city street lit by bright neon signs in vivid colours.
